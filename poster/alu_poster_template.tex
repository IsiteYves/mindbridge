% --- SECTION 1: MAUNICE AKALIZA ---
\block{1. Problem, Context, and Users}{

\begin{itemize}
  \item ALU students struggle with large assignments, overlapping deadlines, 
  and academic stress. Existing platforms such as Canvas provide deadlines 
  but do not break assignments into manageable tasks. 
  An initial survey found that 83\% of respondents experienced moderate to
   very high academic stress. MindBridge addresses this challenge by helping 
   students organize assignments and plan their workload. 
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Recommended pattern:} In \underline{context}, \underline{users} struggle with \underline{problem}. Existing approaches fall short because \underline{gap}. We designed \underline{solution} to improve \underline{outcome}.
}

% --- SECTION 2: MAUNICE AKALIZA ---
\block{2. Objectives and Scope}{
Keep this section tight and measurable.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Objective :} To develop MindBridge, a web-based platform that simplifies
   academic workload management and helps students avoid last-minute assignment pressure.
  \item \textbf{Scope:} The platform enables students to import Canvas assignments, manage deadlines, and automatically convert rubric criteria into smaller, dated milestones. It focuses on improving academic organization and planning among ALU students.
  
\end{itemize}
}

\block{8. Impact, Contribution, and Learning}{
Frame the contribution at three levels.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Technical:} Developed MindBridge to help students manage assignments and academic deadlines.
Designed features to support students during stressful academic periods.
  \item \textbf{User:} Helps students organize their workload and avoid last-minute pressure.
Encourages students to recognize stress and seek support early.
  \item \textbf{Research:} Learned how technology can support students' mental well-being.
Improved our skills in teamwork, software development, testing, and problem-solving.
  \item \textbf{Mission:} Promotes student well-being and makes academic life more manageable in African universities.
\end{itemize}

\takeawaybox{Making academic pressure manageable, one assignment at a time.}
}
